How to Play

A mystery Survivor is secretly chosen from the board.

Ask yes/no questions to eliminate suspects. Only questions that can still narrow the board are shown.

When you think you know the answer, click an active portrait or type the castaway's name. A wrong guess eliminates that player.

Random modes use a fresh board of 24 unique contestants. You can play across all 50 seasons or limit the board to a 10-season era.

Tip: the goal is to solve the mystery with as few questions and wrong guesses as possible.
